# 🚀 Novus UAE — Future Updates & Product Backlog (FUTURE_UPDATES.md)

This document captures strategic features, architectural enhancements, and creative ideas conceptualized during development that are intentionally postponed for future releases.

---

## 📌 Phase 2 & Enterprise Expansion Backlog

### 1. Client Interactive Portal (Executive Dashboard)
- **Concept:** Secure client dashboard where corporate investors and Golden Visa applicants can track real-time government application status (DED approval, Medical Fitness appointment, Emirates ID issuance).
- **Tech Stack:** Next.js App Router protected routes, NextAuth/Auth0, PostgreSQL / Supabase with Row Level Security (RLS).
- **Status:** Postponed to Phase 2 (post-landing MVP launch).

### 2. Multi-Currency Online Deposit & Retainer Payments
- **Concept:** Capability for foreign investors to pay initial government application fees and consultation retainers online via credit card (Stripe, Telr, or Network International UAE) and installment plans (Tabby / Tamara).
- **Status:** Deferred until commercial merchant account and corporate UAE bank account are active.

### 3. AI-Powered UAE Regulation & Visa Advisor (Novus AI Chatbot)
- **Concept:** An autonomous conversational assistant fine-tuned on UAE Commercial Companies Law, ICP Golden Visa criteria, and Free Zone regulations.
- **Capabilities:**
  - Instant qualifying quiz ("Do you qualify for a 10-Year Golden Visa?").
  - Direct WhatsApp lead forwarding with summarized conversation transcripts.
- **Status:** Conceptualized; backlog for Phase 2.

### 4. Interactive Live Document Vault & OCR Scanner
- **Concept:** Enable clients to upload passports, MOA, and trade licenses with automated OCR to extract names, passport numbers, and expiry dates, auto-filling UAE government forms.
- **Status:** Backlog.

### 5. Multi-Branch Physical Presence Locator
- **Concept:** If Novus opens physical executive offices in Abu Dhabi (ADGM), Downtown Dubai, and Riyadh (Saudi expansion), implement an interactive 3D map with bookable boardroom meeting slots.
- **Status:** Backlog.

### 6. Full Multilingual Expansion (Russian, French, Mandarin)
- **Concept:** In addition to English and Arabic, add Russian, Mandarin, and French to target high-net-worth investors from CIS, China, and Europe who represent significant real estate and business formation volume in Dubai.
- **Status:** Deferred until Arabic and English localization are battle-tested.
