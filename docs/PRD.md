# 📄 Novus UAE — Product Requirements Document (PRD.md)

## 1. Product Vision & Executive Summary
Novus is a premier UAE Government Relations and Corporate Services firm operating in Dubai and Abu Dhabi. The digital presence (`novusuae.co`) must serve as an elite, high-converting digital flagship that commands immediate institutional trust from global investors, corporate directors, and High-Net-Worth Individuals (HNWI).

The client objective for this phase is to launch an **Executive Showcase & High-Converting Landing MVP** that allows Novus to begin marketing campaigns and client consultations immediately with maximum visual and technical impact, while preserving a decoupled architecture that allows instant rebranding once final corporate identity assets are delivered.

---

## 2. Target Personas & User Profiles
1. **The International Tech Founder (Alex, 34, UK/Europe):**
   - *Goal:* Needs rapid Dubai Free Zone incorporation (DMCC / IFZA) with 100% foreign ownership and zero corporate tax friction.
   - *Pain Point:* Confused by differing zone fees and hidden government costs.
   - *Novus Solution:* Transparent interactive cost calculator, 3-5 day expedited setup timeline, and instant WhatsApp consultation.
2. **The High-Net-Worth Family Office Director (Tariq, 52, GCC / International):**
   - *Goal:* 10-Year UAE Golden Visa for family and key executives, VIP medical fitness, and corporate bank account structuring.
   - *Pain Point:* Requires absolute confidentiality, bespoke concierge service, and zero bureaucratic delays.
   - *Novus Solution:* Executive leadership credibility, dedicated Relationship Manager (RM), and premium White-Glove VIP PRO handling.
3. **The Global Enterprise Expansion Officer (Elena, 41, Singapore/USA):**
   - *Goal:* Mainland LLC branch setup, Ministry of Economy approvals, and certified MOJ legal translations.
   - *Pain Point:* Complex compliance and labor quotas across MOHRE and DED.
   - *Novus Solution:* Direct government relations interface (Amer, Tasheel, DED) and unified compliance management.

---

## 3. Product Scope & Functional Modules (Phase 1 MVP)
- **Module 1: Luxury Executive Navigation & Brand Bar:** Sticky glassmorphism header, interim Novus monogram, language switcher (EN/AR), consultation booking CTA.
- **Module 2: Hero Section & Trust Authority:** High-impact value proposition, live animated counters (10,000+ Visas, 800+ Companies, 10+ Years, 100% Compliance).
- **Module 3: Interactive UAE Setup Cost Calculator:** 30-second estimator (Jurisdiction, Activity, Visa allocation) with instant quotation dispatch via WhatsApp or email.
- **Module 4: 7 Core Services Bento Hub:** Modern Bento Grid featuring the 7 pillars: Business Setup, Corporate PRO, Golden Visa, Certified Legal Translation, Government Liaison, Document Attestation, Relocation Concierge.
- **Module 5: Leadership & Institutional Values:** Executive profiles (Monir Abueida, Mostafa Hamza, Alaa Salem) and Core Values.
- **Module 6: Client Proof & Institutional Testimonials:** Testimonial carousel and government affiliation trust badges (DED, MOHRE, ICP).
- **Module 7: Lead Capture & Consultation Booking:** Validated form with anti-spam honeypot and direct email dispatch.
- **Module 8: Floating Smart WhatsApp Concierge:** Dynamic context-aware messaging based on active view.
- **Module 9: Executive Footer & Legal Disclosures:** Site architecture, Dubai address, copyright, and regulatory compliance statements.

## 4. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Lighthouse Performance Score:** ≥ 95 on desktop, ≥ 90 on mobile.
- **First Contentful Paint (FCP):** < 1.0s.
- **Largest Contentful Paint (LCP):** < 1.8s.
- **Conversion Rate:** ≥ 8% visitor-to-lead conversion (Form submission or WhatsApp engagement).
- **Calculator Completion Rate:** ≥ 45% of visitors who start the calculator complete it.
